## Full description (en)

Write a letter, give it to a carrier pigeon, and watch it fly across a 3D globe to someone you have never met.

When it lands, they can catch it and reply, and a conversation begins. Every message is translated automatically into each person's language, so you can talk with anyone, anywhere.

• Real people, matched at random in another country, or in a country you choose
• Watch your pigeon fly in real distance and time on a 3D Earth
• Automatic translation in 12 languages, with the original always one tap away
• Voice and video calls with people you chat with
• Your privacy first: only your city is shown, never your exact location
• Safety built in: report and block, 18+ only, links and phone numbers blocked in first messages
• App lock with PIN or fingerprint
• 3 free pigeons every day. Points and VIP unlock fast pigeons, chosen destinations, calls and more

Zajel is for adults 18 and over.

• أمان مدمج: إبلاغ وحظر، للبالغين 18+ فقط، ومنع الروابط وأرقام الهواتف في الرسائل الأولى
• قفل التطبيق برمز أو بصمة
• 3 حمامات مجانية كل يوم، والنقاط و VIP تفتح الحمام السريع واختيار الوجهة والمكالمات وغيرها
