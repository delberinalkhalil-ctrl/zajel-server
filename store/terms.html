Zajel Terms of Use

Last updated: 22 November 2026. Publisher: Delberin. Contact: info@miran-iq.com

Who can use Zajel

You must be 18 or older. One personal account per person.

Your behaviour

No harassment, hate, sexual content involving minors, nudity, scams, spam, requests for money, or sharing other people's private information. Respect people who do not want to continue a conversation.

Your content

You are responsible for what you send. Messages are machine-translated, and translations can be wrong. We may remove content and suspend or delete accounts that break these terms.

Safety

Use Block and Report in the app. Never send money to someone you met on Zajel. See our child safety standards and privacy policy.

The service

Zajel is provided as is and may change or stop. It is currently free of charge.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings in the app.
