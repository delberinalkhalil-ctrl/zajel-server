## What we collect
- **Account:** email address, display name, year of birth (to confirm you are 18+), chosen language, and an optional profile photo and short bio.
- **Approximate location:** your city and country, and a position rounded to about 10 km, used to send pigeons between places and show distances. We never store or show your exact location.
- **Content you send:** pigeon letters, chat messages and their machine translations.
- **Calls:** voice and video are relayed live through our calling provider (Agora) and are **not recorded**. We keep call metadata (who, when, duration, type).
- **Purchases:** purchase tokens and order IDs from Google Play, to grant points and VIP. We do not receive your card details.
- **Device:** a push notification token, and basic technical logs for security and debugging.
- **Safety:** reports you make or receive, and block lists.

## How we use it
To run the service (matching pigeons, delivering and translating messages, calls, purchases), to keep people safe (moderation, reports, preventing scams and underage use), and to send notifications you can turn off.

## Who processes it for us
Google Firebase and Google Cloud (hosting, database, authentication, notifications, translation), Agora (calls), Google Play (payments). They process data only on our behalf.

## What other users see
Your display name, photo, bio, language, country and (unless you turn it off) city. Never your email, year of birth or exact location.

## Retention and deletion
We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in **Me → Delete account permanently**, or by emailing [SUPPORT EMAIL]. This deletes your profile, photo, pigeons, chats and messages. Purchase records may be kept as required by law.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to processing. Contact [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## Children
Zajel is only for people aged 18 and over. We remove accounts that we learn belong to minors.

## Security
Data is encrypted in transit. Access is limited by security rules, and only our servers can change accounts, points and messages.

## Changes
We will post updates here and notify you in the app when the changes are significant.
